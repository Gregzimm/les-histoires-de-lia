Privacy Policy

Application: lia-pipeline — Les Histoires de LIA
Last updated: May 12, 2026

1. Overview

This application ("lia-pipeline") is a personal automation tool that publishes children's story videos to social media platforms (TikTok, Instagram, YouTube) on behalf of the account owner. It is not a public-facing product and does not collect data from end users.

2. Data We Access

When authorized by the account owner, this application accesses:

  • TikTok account identifier and display information (to authenticate the publishing account)
  • Permission to upload and publish video content to the authorized TikTok account

3. How We Use the Data

The data accessed is used solely to:

  • Authenticate the application with TikTok on behalf of the account owner
  • Upload and publish video content to the account owner's TikTok profile

We do not sell, share, or transfer any data to third parties.

4. Data Storage

Access tokens are stored securely as encrypted environment variables in GitHub Actions secrets. No personal data is stored in databases or shared with any third party.

5. Data Retention

Access tokens are retained only as long as needed to operate the automation. They can be revoked at any time through the TikTok account settings.

6. Your Rights

You may revoke access at any time by visiting TikTok Connected Apps Settings and removing the lia-pipeline application.
